Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

RainCheck is a browser extension that helps you monitor your Claude.ai session and weekly usage, and generate a handoff summary of a conversation so you can continue it elsewhere.

Data We Collect

RainCheck does not collect, transmit, or sell any data to us. There is no server, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing. We do not operate any backend, so there is nowhere for your data to be stored or sent.

How the Extension Works

The extension only ever interacts with Claude.ai on your own logged-in session, using the same APIs the Claude.ai web app itself uses. While you are on claude.ai, RainCheck may:

  • Read your session usage (5-hour and weekly utilization percentages) and your current conversation (messages and any generated files) from Claude.ai's own API, so it can display usage bars and build a handoff summary.
  • Show a floating panel with the recovered conversation and a "Copy Markdown" / "Copy Summary" action.

All of this is limited to the claude.ai website you are already logged into, requires no extra permissions beyond accessing that site, and the data is used only to render the extension's own UI in your browser.

Where Your Data Lives

Everything stays local to your browser. RainCheck does not send your conversation, messages, usage, or any other personal information to any external server other than Claude.ai itself — and only to fetch the same data the Claude.ai page you opened already loads. No data is stored on our servers (we have none).

Permissions

RainCheck requests access only to https://claude.ai/* (and *.claude.ai/*), which is necessary for the extension to function. It uses the downloads capability only if you choose to save a file, and storage is not used to collect personal data.

Third-Party Services

RainCheck is not affiliated with Anthropic or Claude.ai. Any data you have on Claude.ai is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms. RainCheck does not transmit your data to any other third party.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, and continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
