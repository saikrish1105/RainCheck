Never lose your work when Claude.ai hits a rate limit.

RainCheck is a lightweight Chrome extension that shows your Claude.ai session and weekly usage, and lets you export a clean markdown summary so you can continue your conversation anywhere — even after an interruption.

Features

Two simple things. Everything you need.

RainCheck focuses on the two moments that matter: knowing where you stand, and saving your work before it's gone.

📊

Usage bars

See your 5-hour session and 7-day weekly usage with progress bars and a countdown to the next reset. Always know how much room you have left.

☁️

One-click handoff

Press a button to turn your current conversation into a clean, ready-to-paste markdown transcript — title, summary, and your messages in order.

📄

Keep your files

Generated files Claude created are included in the transcript as code blocks, so nothing you made is left behind.

🔒

Private by design

Everything runs locally in your browser using your own Claude.ai login. No accounts, no tracking, nothing sent to any third party.

How it works

From "waiting 5 hours" to "continued instantly."

When a session is interrupted, you no longer have to start over. Here's the flow.

Install & open Claude.ai

A small, cute orange cloud floats on the page. Drag it anywhere you like.

Check your usage

Session and weekly bars keep you aware of how much of your limit you've used.

Generate a summary

Hit Generate Summary to turn the conversation into a clean markdown transcript.

Continue anywhere

Copy the markdown and paste it into another AI or session to pick up exactly where you left off.

Ready to give it a try? The extension is free and takes a minute to install. We're in the final steps of publishing to the Chrome Web Store — the install link will appear here the moment it's live.
