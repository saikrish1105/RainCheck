RainCheck

Recover your work when a Claude.ai session is interrupted.

Behavior

Optional: AI handoff summary v2 · opt-in

RainCheck builds a structured (no-AI) report for free. If you want a natural-language handoff summary, you can point it at your own API key below. This is entirely optional and off by default. Your key is stored only in this extension's local storage.

Privacy

Everything is processed locally in your browser. Recovered artifacts and transcripts are stored only in chrome.storage.local and never sent anywhere. The only thing that can leave your device is an AI-summary request, and only if you enable it with your own key.
